Quick Capture showed nothing

- The overlay is a frameless, always-on-top 44x44 shape with no title bar, so
  any throw left a blank window with no way to tell that anything had gone
  wrong. It is now wrapped in an error boundary that shows the message where it
  can be seen, with Try again and Reset.
- The booster panel no longer renders an empty box for a missing or empty
  series; it says which series and what to do. Stats tolerate a missing series
  instead of throwing and blanking everything.

Channels are now visible

- A new panel on the Classes screen showing every channel the engine reads: how
  far each has been read, how much the model has understood, how many classes
  and links came out of it, and when it was last read.
- Per channel: read it now, and pause or resume it. A summary bar with the
  total read and a progress bar per connection.
- The channels with no public web preview are named explicitly, with what is
  needed to read them, instead of silently reading nothing.

The engine can start itself

- Settings previously said to open the other app and left you there. It now
  launches the engine and waits for it to answer, and says precisely what
  failed if it cannot.

The timetable

- The offer only appeared on a completely empty planner, so anyone who had
  added one commitment by hand never saw it. It now always appears: it loads
  the eleven classes when empty, offers to add only the missing ones when
  partial, and confirms when complete. The merge is idempotent and never
  overwrites something edited by hand.

Also removed the unused-mut warning on the sidecar, and made the stat fields in
the overlay tolerate a missing series.
